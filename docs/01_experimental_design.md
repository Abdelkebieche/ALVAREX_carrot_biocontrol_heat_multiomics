# Experimental design

## Biological scope

The study separates three analytical layers:

1. **Disease phenotype:** six carrot genotypes (DEEP, NANT, NEVA, OXHELLA, PRESTO, ROBILA), four thermal regimes (NH, HS-7, HS-2, HS), Control/Treated, four biological replicate pots per Genotype × Temperature × Treatment combination.
2. **RNA-seq:** PRESTO and ROBILA only, NH/HS-7/HS-2, sampled at D0, D2 and D4. The WGCNA networks use D2+D4; maSigPro uses D0+D2+D4.
3. **UHPLC-HRMS:** PRESTO and ROBILA only, NH/HS-7/HS-2, sampled at D0, D4 and D10.

## Timeline

- **D0:** pre-treatment sampling, then water or *Bacillus pumilus* QST 2808 product application.
- **D2:** RNA sampling immediately before *Alternaria dauci* inoculation.
- **D4:** RNA + metabolomics sampling, two days after inoculation.
- **D10:** metabolomics sampling.
- **D11 onward:** disease scoring at 11, 18, 26, 32, 40, 47 and 54 DAI.

## Temperature regimes

- **NH:** non-heated.
- **HS-7:** heat exposure initiated seven days before D0.
- **HS-2:** heat exposure initiated two days before D0.
- **HS:** heat initiated at D0; phenotype only in this repository/manuscript.

The heated regimes represent distinct thermal histories rather than a simple duration series.
